Predictive Health — Scoring & Model Reference

Two-Tier Hybrid Engine

Every monitored entity is scored locally with zero external dependencies using two complementary tiers: Tier-1 Physics & Peer Heuristics (optical decay slopes, laser bias rise, pre-FEC/post-FEC BER, RoCEv2 PFC/ECN/HOQ asymmetry, localized thermal/fan/PSU and control-plane anomalies) combined with Tier-2 Gradient-Boosted Trees (GBDT) trained on physics-grounded trajectories and calibrated against anonymized fabric baselines.

Severity & Horizon Bands

CRITICAL (score ≥ 70, 24h horizon) indicates imminent or active failure requiring immediate triage. WARNING (score 40–69, 48–72h horizon) flags developing degradation to address in the next maintenance window. INFO (score < 40) entities are nominal and omitted from the actionable table.

Early Precursors (Sub-Threshold)

The EARLY badge marks entities flagged as WARNING/CRITICAL while all underlying raw LLDPq domain analyzers (optical, BER, flap, fabric-check, hardware, BGP, EVPN-MH, routes, duplicate, logs, system watchdog) still report nominal/healthy status.
